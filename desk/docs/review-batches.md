## Review batches

- After reviewing a batch, type `quiz` in the desk session. It asks up to
  three questions from cached level-2 summaries and points back to the
  relevant Review when you miss one or are unsure. Only the daily completed
  quiz count is written to the queue store.
- To read back a marked paper export, use `/inbox import <scan.pdf>` or type
  `import <scan.pdf>` in the desk session. Keep the printed set number,
  item IDs, and option numbers visible. The preview identifies unreadable
  marks by item and page. Type `confirm scan import` after checking the
  displayed answers and verbatim comments. If the queue changes, review the
  new preview before confirming.
